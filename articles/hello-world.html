Hello, world: why I’m starting to write

Oct 31, 2026 · Andrei Stanescu · 2 min read

I spend most of my days helping a team of engineers do the best work of their careers — and somewhere along the way I realised I was having the same conversations over and over, without ever writing any of them down. This blog is my attempt to fix that.

What to expect

Mostly notes from the trenches of engineering leadership: what works (and what doesn’t) when it comes to coaching, delivery, hiring, and building teams that people actually want to be part of. I’ve also spent the past year going deep on AI-assisted workflows, so expect a fair share of thoughts on where those tools genuinely help and where the hype outruns the value.

Besides my own writing, I want this to be a place where I collect articles and ideas worth your time — things that changed how I think about a problem, or that I keep recommending in 1:1s and study groups.

Why now

Writing forces clarity. If I can’t explain an idea in plain words, I probably don’t understand it well enough yet. If you find something here useful, or you disagree with it, my inbox is open.
